# BusinessCard

Business card 85 × 55 mm (preview at twice the size). Front G100 with the mark on the pattern, back White with the wordmark and contact details.

- Name in IBM Plex Sans SemiBold 9 pt, role in Plex Sans 6.5 pt `text-secondary`, contact in Plex Mono 6 pt. The domain in `link-primary`.
- 3 mm bleed, 4 mm safety margin. Print: front full bleed `#161616`, star as a spot color (a Pantone close to Purple 60) or CMYK.
- Name and contact details are placeholders until the new surname is settled. The brand stays the same.
