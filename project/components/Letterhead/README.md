# Letterhead

A4 letterhead following the DIN 5008 Form B layout, always in the White (paper) theme.

- Wordmark top left, 9 mm high, 25 mm from the left edge. Sender block on the right in `legal-01`, `text-secondary`.
- Address window 45 mm from the top, 85 × 45 mm, return address line above it in 7 pt `text-secondary`.
- Fold marks at 105 and 210 mm, punch mark at 148.5 mm, in `border-strong-01`.
- Footer in three columns: contact, bank, tax. Headings in SemiBold, sentence case, no all caps.
- Text in IBM Plex Sans 10 pt, subject SemiBold 11 pt. No Purple except the star in the logo.
- Replace every placeholder in square brackets. The personal name is swapped after the name change, the brand stays.
