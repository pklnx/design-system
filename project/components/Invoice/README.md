# Invoice

Invoice template based on the letterhead, White (paper) theme. The invoice itself is German.

- Invoice details to the right of the address window: invoice number, date, service period, customer number. Labels in `text-secondary`, numbers in IBM Plex Mono.
- Title as a prompt `$ rechnung`, the `$` in `star`. The only place with Purple apart from the logo.
- Table in Carbon style: header row on `layer-01` in SemiBold, rows with a `border-subtle-00` hairline, amounts right aligned with tabular figures.
- Check the mandatory details under §14 UStG (German VAT Act): name and address of both parties, tax number or VAT ID, date, sequential number, quantity and type of service, service period, net amount, tax rate and tax amount or the note on §19 UStG.
- From 2027/2028 an e-invoice (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD) may be required. This template is the visual rendering, not the structured format.
- The items in the preview are examples with placeholders.
