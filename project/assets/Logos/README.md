# Logos

- `pklnx-wordmark-dark.svg`: text and bracket in `logo.dark.ink`, star in `logo.dark.star` (Purple 50). On the G100 ground.
- `pklnx-wordmark-light.svg`: text and bracket in `logo.light.ink`, star in `logo.light.star` (Purple 60). On White.
- `pklnx-wordmark-black.svg` / `-white.svg`: single color for stamps, engraving, fax, vinyl.
- `pklnx-mark-dark.svg`, `-light.svg`, `-black.svg`, `-white.svg`: the mark, same colors as above.
- `pklnx-favicon.svg`: 64 × 64 tile on the dark ground (`logo.dark.ground`), 22 % corners. Use as `favicon.svg`, also export a 32 px PNG and a 180 px Apple touch icon.
- `pklnx-avatar.svg`: circle on the dark ground for GitHub, LinkedIn, Gravatar.
- Wordmark from IBM Plex Mono (Bold and Regular), converted to paths. The files need no fonts. The colours are the `logo` values in `theme/brand.json`, the shapes are in `theme/logo-geometry.json`. Generated by `scripts/build_logo.py`.
- `products/`: the marks, lockups and app icons of mail-archive and paperless-go, see `products/README.md`. Generated by `scripts/build_product_logos.py` and `scripts/render_pngs.mjs`. Indexed in this group as `products/<file>` (SVG and PNG) and uploaded to the claude.ai design system.
